Why True Ceylon Cinnamon Matters

Over 90% of supermarket cinnamon is Cassia from China or Indonesia, which contains high coumarin levels toxic to the liver. True Ceylon Cinnamon is completely safe for daily use.

✅ True Ceylon Cinnamon (Sri Lanka)

  • Near-Zero Coumarin: Less than 0.004%. Safe for everyday consumption.
  • Thin Multi-Layers: Soft, delicate quills rolled like fine parchment.
  • Subtle Sweet Taste: Mildly floral, never pungent, spicy or harsh.
  • Proven Health Perks: Naturally assists blood sugar & glycemic balance.

⚠️ Cassia Cinnamon (China / Indonesia)

  • High Coumarin Content: Up to 1%, posing liver and kidney strain.
  • Thick, Hollow Bark: Hard, dense single hollow quills like tree bark.
  • Sharp, Harsh Burn: Bitter peppery burn that overpowers recipes.
  • Industrial Production: Often chemically fumigated and bleached.
